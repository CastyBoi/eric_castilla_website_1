The Arc

How I got here, and where I want to go.

I got my Mechanical Engineering degree from a sister school of the University of Texas where I also studied reactor engineering and nuclear physics. One thing I've learned over the years, is that the art of problem solving is applicable anywhere, the challenge is knowing where to apply it.

My first job was as an Automation Technician at Intel, diagnosing and repairing automated production equipment to minimize downtime, along with teaching small teams about Prevenative Maintaince. This role was fun, working under pressure I kind of enjoy. However, I really wanted to put my degree to good use, and see what else was out there.

From there I moved to Crafco as a Design Engineer and Project Engineer. This invovled things like: Lean events, time studies, CAD floor plans, ROI analysis for new equipment. After a couple years, and lots of learnig, I was able to have a new postion created for me.

I didn't know what an Analytics Engineering was when I was in school, but now I guess I am one. I started this role right as AI coding tools got really good. I leaned into it, and was able to successfully meld my existing knowledge into a space where I could see a business problem, and actually ship the thing that improved or solved it.

I've never claimed to be an expert, and I certainly know that I rely a little too much on Claude/ChatGPT. However, all of us have access to these tools now, and I think what sets me apart is understanding that the code was never the issue. Instead, I have been focusing on the people side, getting everyone educated and aligned so we can actually deliver things of value to the business.
